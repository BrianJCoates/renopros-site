Our Commercial Specialty

White Box Build-Outs

A white box is a commercial space delivered as a clean, code-ready blank canvas — finished enough for a tenant to move in and add their own brand and finishes, but without the custom fit-out. It's the fastest way for a landlord to make a space leasable, and for a tenant to start their build-out on solid footing. It sits between a bare shell and a full custom interior — and it's what we do best.

A typical white box from us includes

  • Demising walls framed, insulated, drywalled, taped and primed
  • Suspended ceiling grid with standard lighting
  • HVAC distribution and controls
  • Electrical service, panel, and outlets roughed to code
  • Sealed concrete floor, ready for the tenant's finish
  • ADA-compliant restroom(s)
  • Fire sprinkler coverage and life-safety systems
  • Finished storefront, entry doors, and exit signage

How we deliver

  1. Walkthrough & scopeWe assess the shell and define exactly what "white box" means for your space and lease.
  2. Permits & drawingsWe handle the paperwork and keep it code-compliant.
  3. BuildOne accountable commercial contractor, one clear timeline.
  4. Inspections & handoffWe deliver a clean, inspected, tenant-ready space.

Landlords, developers, and property managers across Michigan trust Renovation Pros to turn shells into leasable space on schedule. Licensed, insured, and 25 years in commercial contracting.
